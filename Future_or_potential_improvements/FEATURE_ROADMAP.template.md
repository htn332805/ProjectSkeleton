## 1) Roadmap principles

- Roadmap items are hypotheses, not promises.
- Prefer small increments that can be shipped and validated.
- Separate “feature work” from “refactor work,” but track dependencies.
- Prioritize by user impact and risk reduction.

## 5) Prioritization rubric

Score 1–5 for each:
- User impact
- Revenue/value impact (if applicable)
- Risk reduction
- Effort (inverse)
- Strategic alignment

Priority guidance:
- High impact + low effort → do sooner
- High risk reduction → do soon
- Unclear value → run a small experiment first
